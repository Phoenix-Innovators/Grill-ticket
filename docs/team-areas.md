## How we work

Each owner researches their area, chooses an approach within scope, estimates effort, and creates or refines their issues. Each issue has one responsible owner, a clear outcome, dependencies, and two or three checks that show it works.

Record decisions and evidence with the issue. Review changes together when they affect other roles, cost, access, or product behavior. Brie coordinates the work; each owner remains responsible for their own research and delivery.

See the [ticket standard](mvp.md#owner-responsibilities-and-ticket-standard).
